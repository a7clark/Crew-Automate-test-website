FAQ

Frequently asked questions

What is the AI Automation Society?
AIS is the largest community for people learning and building with AI automation. We bring together builders, operators, founders, and freelancers who want to use AI tools to save time, grow their businesses, and stay ahead of the curve. You get workflows, courses, live events, and a private community — all in one place.
Do I need to be technical to join?
Not at all. AIS is designed for people at every skill level. Whether you're a complete beginner or a seasoned developer, you'll find content and community relevant to where you are. Our beginner learning paths require zero coding experience.
What tools does AIS cover?
We cover the full AI automation stack — Make (Integromat), n8n, Zapier, ChatGPT, Claude, custom GPTs, AI agents, Airtable, Notion automations, and more. We add new tool coverage constantly based on what the community is using.
Can I switch plans at any time?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade your plan at any time. Upgrades take effect immediately. Downgrades take effect at the end of your current billing period, and we keep all your data safe.
What kind of support is available?
Free users get documentation and a community forum. Pro users get email support with a 24-hour SLA. Business users get dedicated Slack support, a named customer success manager, and priority response times.
Do you offer discounts for nonprofits or startups?
Yes. We offer 50% off all paid plans for registered nonprofits and early-stage startups (under 2 years old, under $1M ARR). Contact us at hello@crewautomateapp.com with proof of eligibility.
